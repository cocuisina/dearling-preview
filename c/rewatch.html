The harbour on SundaysTold by Arthur, March 2026
  1. We had nothing, but every Sunday we took the ferry across to the harbour.
  2. Your grandmother wore the blue dress, and I carried the basket up Mill Street.
  3. By the time your father was ten, he was the one counting the boats.
  4. I would tell it the same way again. That was the best of us.
